Frequently Asked Questions on Book Spine Width & Cover Layout

How do you calculate exact book spine width?
The industry formula is: Spine Width = (Page Count / 2) × (Paper Weight in GSM / 1000) × Paper Bulk + Glue Hinge (0.5 mm). Example: 240 pages on 80 gsm bulk 1.3 paper yields 120 sheets × 0.080 × 1.3 + 0.5 = 12.98 mm (approx. 13.0 mm).
What does Paper Bulk / Volume mean?
Paper bulk is the thickness-to-weight ratio (caliper in microns divided by grammage). High-bulk book papers (bulk 1.3 - 1.5) create thicker books without adding weight, while glossy coated papers are compact (0.85 - 0.95).
What is the minimum spine thickness for readable text?
The industry threshold is at least 6 mm (0.24 in). Between 3 and 5 mm, text is possible with strict centering tolerances (±1 mm). Under 3 mm, spine text should be avoided and saddle-stitching is recommended.
What bleed margin is required for full wrap-around covers?
Commercial printing and print-on-demand platforms (Amazon KDP, IngramSpark) require a mandatory 3 mm (0.125 in) bleed on all 4 outer edges (adding 6 mm total to flat width and height).
