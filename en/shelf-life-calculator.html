Frequently Asked Questions on Shelf-Life & Export Dates

FAQ
Why does Canada (CFIA) use two-letter month codes like "FE" or "AL"?
Canadian regulations (Safe Food for Canadians Regulations — SFCR) mandate standardized bilingual month codes (English/French) on "Best Before / Meilleur avant" dates to avoid ambiguity: JA, FE, MR, AL, MA, JN, JL, AU, SE, OC, NO, DE. For example AL stands for both April and for Avril, e JN for June/Juin.
What is the difference between USA (FDA) and European (EU) date formats?
In Italy and the European Union (EU Reg. 1169/2011) the date follows GG/MM/AAAA (es. 05/08/2027 = August 5). In the United States the numeric order is reversed to MM/DD/YYYY (08/05/2027): for evitare contestazioni doganali o confusione nei consumatori USA è buona prassi affiancare o preferire la forma testuale AUG 05 2027.
What do Gulf Countries (GCC / SFDA) require on food labels?
GSO technical standards (GSO 9 / GSO 150 for Arabia Saudita/SFDA, Emirati Arabi Uniti, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain) richiedono obbligatoriamente both dates in plain text: production date (PROD) and expiry date (EXP), in format DD/MM/YYYY if shelf life is under 3 months or MM/YYYY if over 3 months. Dates must be printed or embossed directly on the original packaging (post-print adhesive stickers are strictly prohibited) and accompanied by Arabic wording.
Quali regole valgono for il Regno Unito (UK / FSA post-Brexit)?
In the United Kingdom (Food Standards Agency — FSA) the standard format is DD/MM/YYYY. For long-shelf-life products (> 3 months) where the day is omitted, you must specify Best before end MM/YYYY o End of MM/YYYY. Furthermore, post-Brexit, the label must state the business name and physical address of a Food Business Operator (FBO) established in the UK.
How do dates work in Japan and what is the "One-Third Rule"?
In Japan (Consumer Affairs Agency / JAS) the format follows descending order AAAA.MM.GG (o AAAA年MM月DD日). Regulations strictly distinguish between Shoumi-kigen (賞味期限) for la data preferibile (TMC) e Shouhi-kigen (消費期限) for la scadenza tassativa dei freschi (≤5 days). Nella grande distribuzione giapponese vige la ferrea One-Third commercial rule (1/3 rule): the product must reach the distributor within the first third of its total shelf life, otherwise the shipment is rejected.
What is the difference between "Use By" and "Best Before"?
La Use By Date (Use By) applies to microbiologically perishable foods and indicates a strict food safety limit. Minimum Durability / Best Before (Best Before) indicates until when the product retains optimal sensory qualities if stored properly.
